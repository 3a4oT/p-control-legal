Deleting your p-control account

Version 1.0 · Effective 13 September 2026

This page is about p-control, the Android TV parental-control app published by Petro Rovenskyi. It explains how to delete your account, what is deleted with it, what is kept and for how long, and the three cases where we will refuse.

01 How to delete your account

You do it yourself, from the account it belongs to. No email to us is required, and we do not ask you why.

  1. Sign in at control.rovenskyi.com.
  2. Open Account (control.rovenskyi.com/account).
  3. At the bottom of that page, in its own zone below everything else, press Close account. It sits apart from the ordinary settings on purpose: it is the only act on that screen that cannot be undone.
  4. Read what it tells you it will close. Before anything is deleted you are shown which households go with the account — those are the ones where you are the only member left.
  5. Confirm with your password. We ask for it again even though you are already signed in, because a session left open on a shared computer must not be able to end somebody’s account and their household with it.
Deletion is immediate. There is no waiting period and no recovery window: the records described in §2 are removed when you confirm, not queued for later. If you want a copy of anything, take it first.

02 What is deleted

Closing the account removes, permanently:

  • Your account itself — your email address, your password hash, your language, your sign-in methods, and the link to your Telegram chat if you had connected one.
  • Every browser session you had open, and what was recorded with each one.
  • Each household where you were the only member, and everything inside it: the children’s profiles and their names, the screens you had paired, every rule, schedule and limit, the record of what was watched and for how long, the approval requests, the alerts, and the list of apps each screen had reported.
  • The credentials your televisions used. Each paired screen’s broker account is revoked as part of the same operation, so a device that is switched on afterwards cannot reach the service at all.

Households where other people remain are not deleted — they are not yours alone to end. You are removed from them, and they carry on without you. If you were the last person there who could manage members, see §4.

03 What is kept, and for how long

One thing survives the deletion of an account, and only for accounts it applies to:

DataKept for
Operator audit records. If this account was used to act inside somebody else’s household as an operator of the service, the record of what it did there remains. That record is not ours to erase: it exists so the people whose household was touched can find out who touched it, which is the carve-out GDPR Article 17(3) makes for records kept to establish or defend legal claims Kept. An account with such a record cannot be deleted through the cabinet at all — see §4

Everything else in a household is already on a clock of its own while the account is open, and those limits apply whether or not you ever delete anything:

DataKept for
Hour-by-hour detail of what was watchedAt most 90 days, and less if your plan’s history window is shorter — then deleted automatically, leaving only the daily totals
Daily totals, approval requests, alerts, and what a screen reported about itselfYour plan’s history window — 90 days on the current plan — then deleted automatically
A pairing code, and the temporary credential issued with it10 minutes, then expired automatically; the expired record is deleted a week later

The full retention table, including what the app never collects in the first place, is in the Privacy Policy.

04 When we cannot delete it

The cabinet tells you which of these applies before you type your password, rather than failing afterwards.

  • You are the last person who can manage members in a household where others remain. Deleting you would leave a household nobody can administer, and that household is not yours alone to end. Hand the permission to another member first, or remove the others, and then close the account.
  • The account holds operator audit records (§3). Write to us at the address below and we will handle it by hand, keeping only what Article 17(3) requires.
  • It is the demonstration account used by Google Play’s review team. It is protected so that a review is not broken by a deletion nobody expected.

05 Deleting some data without closing the account

You do not have to close the account to get rid of data. From the cabinet you can unpair a screen, which removes its app list and the credentials it was using; remove a child’s profile, which takes their rules and their name with it; or delete a household outright while keeping your account. Each of those is immediate in the same way.
